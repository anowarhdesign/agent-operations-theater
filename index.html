    The Scene Engine

    You describe a scene in a JSON recipe, and the engine builds it beautifully, animates it and makes it interactive.

    The book
    The layout math: spacing, alignment, geometry and light. The orange rings.
    The reader
    Reads the JSON recipe card and works out what goes where.
    The renderer
    Draws point clouds, images and motion along a timeline, wearing our shell design.
    The wiring
    Clicks, live data and connections to other systems. The moving dots.
    Teaching a scene
    Gather the ingredients, cut the picture into layers, spread them in 3D, swap in simple then detailed shapes, then texture and light. Open the big screen to watch it.

    The desks

    Each desk has its own agent and its own custom interface. Click any desk, monitor or agent to zoom inside its screen and watch what it is building.
